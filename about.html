Designed for better living

Decoture brings together refined design, functional furniture and carefully chosen imported pieces for homes, offices and studios in Bangladesh.

Décor, cut like couture

The name joins décor and couture. It stands for design, individuality and attention to detail.

We believe a good space is not only about how it looks, but how it makes everyday living feel. That is why we choose each piece for its proportions, its materials and the way it will be used, then present it with clear and honest information.

Starting with the chair

Our first collection is imported seating: executive, ergonomic, task and training chairs.

Every chair is built with fully foreign fittings. The mechanism, gas lift, base and wheels are covered by a 2-year warranty on almost every model, and we keep deep stock in Dhaka so an entire office can be furnished in matching chairs.

Furniture for living rooms, bedrooms and dining, home décor, and interior design services will follow.
